Founded in 2010 by Debraj Sanyal and Ishita Das Sanyal in Kolkata, DOS has pioneered scientific paranormal research across India under the banner "Rise Above Fear".

THE SCIENTIFIC APPROACH

Unlike sensationalized media representations, DOS approaches every unexplained report with skepticism and scientific rigor. Over 95% of reported hauntings are found to be natural occurrences caused by carbon monoxide, electromagnetic interference from faulty wiring, structural resonance infrasound (18.9 Hz), or psychological suggestion.

The remaining 5% of unexplained anomalies are captured on multi-sensor telemetry, thermal imaging, and calibrated audio arrays for exhaustive forensic analysis.
